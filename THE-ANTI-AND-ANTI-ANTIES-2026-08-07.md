## The rule

```text
BROWN        the thing itself           (0 turns)
ANTI         BROWN turned by -1/3       (a third of the wheel, not a flip)
ANTI-ANTI    turned by -1/3 again       (-2/3 in all)
close        -1/3 x 3 = -1 = one whole  (three thirds return to the start)
```
