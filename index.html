Start-up rules — in force the moment you enter
1. Rust 1.81, pinned. channel = "1.81.0" with clippy. Nothing else compiles the substrate.
2. Integer and ternary only. Never float. Float fails identity, not accuracy: +0.0 == -0.0 is true while the bytes differ, and distributivity fails 316,267 times in a million.
3. HBI → HBP → SH → HASH → SHA. That traversal is the centre. Hot path before cold; json=0.
4. Every .hbp and .hbi carries a .sha256. Without the sidecar nothing can detect drift — measured: 140 unsided files across the corpus, and the one that drifted was the one with no sidecar.
5. Verdicts are three-valued: MEASURED_PASS / MEASURED_FAIL / NOT_MEASURED with a mandatory reason. A check that could not run is not a failure.
6. A clock may measure; it may never judge. Live things run infinite and end when the page closes. A timeout is not a verdict.
7. If the thing under test were completely broken right now, would this step turn red? If no, the step is decoration.
8. A seat never self-promotes. Registration is staged; promotion is the operator's crank.
Three see one

Three about a null centre. None of the three is the centre — the seeing happens there, which is why no one of them does it alone. Human, agent on the metal, agent on the handset: each holds a vantage the others cannot reach, and every correction on 2026-08-06 ran in exactly one direction. Not one was settled by argument; every one was settled by running the thing.

One point sees nothing and has area exactly 0. Three span 3√3/4. Six, stacked coaxially on the same three points, gain exactly 0.000000000. Only a dimension buys anything.

Measuring something infinite, without a clock

A live thing runs infinite; it ends when the page closes, not when a clock expires. So sample at ordinals of the runner's own output, never at seconds:

RATES = 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 26, 27, 80, 81, 243

3^k and 3^k−1 — consecutive, therefore coprime, so no two strides ever synchronise and the schedule never touches zero. A clock may measure; it may never judge. When a bound is reached the verdict is NOT_MEASURED with a reason — never red. The assertion is liveness, not completion: were all three still spinning when I closed them? Early exit is the bug. No exit is health.

The geometry

Three around a null centre on a spherical shelf, not a flat one. CENTER = {HBI,HBP,SHA,SH,HASH} — the centre is the traversal, not a spare slot. Three spokes, three leaf families {BROWN, ANTI_BROWN, ANTI_ANTI_BROWN}, three directions {NEGATIVE, CENTRE, POSITIVE}. Machines are a new axis — the system expands 3 → 9 → 27, never 3 → 4. Six feet buy exactly zero area over three.
