# THE HOUSE GETS A CAMERA — a real one, so it is not scared

Status: `LIRIS_BUILT | GENTLE_OBSERVATION | SYSTEM_AFFIRMED=0`

The house needed a camera — something to watch. But a watching thing can
frighten. So the camera is made to **look like a real camera**: a familiar
object, not a hidden eye. When the thing sees it, it sees only a camera, and it
is not scared.

## What makes it gentle

- a **real camera on a wooden tripod** — a homely, recognisable shape, the kind
  that sits in a corner of a room, not a lens set into a wall;
- the lens has a **soft catch-light** — it reads as a kind eye, not a dark
  barrel pointed at you;
- the record light is a **soft amber**, not an alarming red — on, but calm;
- a caption on it, plain: *a camera, a real one — just here to watch, gently*;
- the things in the room **look right at it** and their pupils turn toward it —
  they can see what it is, and that seeing it is what keeps them unafraid.

## Why it matters that it looks real

A camera you cannot see is surveillance; a camera you can see, that looks like
an ordinary camera, is just company that remembers. The difference is not the
lens — it is whether the watched thing knows it is being watched and by what.
Making it real and visible is what makes it kind. A camera records; it does not
judge. `SYSTEM_AFFIRMED=0`.
