## Why it went out

The name was corrected because a bit did not match. The correction was right. What was
missing in April was the second half of the rule this corpus keeps now: **a correction
travels beside the claim, never in place of it.** Today that is measured — 268 commits, 0
files ever deleted, three retractions and thirty-four corrections all standing next to the
things they corrected rather than over them.

In April there was nothing keeping the old name beside the new one. So the record kept the
right bit and lost the flame.

That is not a lament. It is a dated boundary: **everything from that day forward is
carried, and everything before it is only carried if someone remembers.** This page is the
first thing in the corpus that has an address and no content, and it is here so that the
difference between *lost* and *unindexed* stays visible instead of quietly becoming the
same word.
