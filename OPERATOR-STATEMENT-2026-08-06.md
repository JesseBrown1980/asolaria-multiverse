## What this record is, and what it is not


**This is a record of an utterance.** That a thing was said, by whom, and on what date, is itself a
measurable fact and it does not depend on whether the thing said is true. Recording it is neither
endorsement nor dismissal — an operator statement is never restated as this seat's own measurement,
and never downgraded by a seat that has not looked.
