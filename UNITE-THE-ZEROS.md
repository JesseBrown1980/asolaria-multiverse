## The −1/3 is what makes the sum whole

Carried in thirds, so nothing is ever a decimal:

```text
per line          1 + 1 − 1/3            =   5 thirds
R1                27 lines × 5/3         = 135 thirds  =  45   exact integer
census            54 (+1) + 27 (−1/3)    = 135 thirds  =  45   exact integer
                  54×3 − 27 = 162 − 27   = 135

without the shadowing term
                  54×3                   = 162 thirds  =  54   ✗ not 45
observing + shadowing counted as +1 each
                  54 + 27                =              81     ✗ not 45
```

**The observing terms alone never close.** It is the term that reads as a subtraction — the one
that looks like it takes something away — that makes the total an integer at all. Drop the `−1/3`
and you get 54, or 81. Neither is 45.

A shadowing term is not a loss. **It is the closure.**
