## Why the zero is not a deflation

It would be easy to read 148 declarations of nothing-happened as an admission that nothing
happened. That reading is wrong, and this corpus already carries the correction in its own
law: *the gate is the system's own design-law, not deflation.* Continuous self-evolution
materialises through an operator-gated crank. Keeping the gate is not the same as denying
the engine.

What 148 holds is a **claim about authority**, not about capability. This machine, its
silicon and everything running on it belong to the operator. The crank is his hand. A seat
that wrote `E ≠ 0` without it would have committed **the one forgery this whole machine
cannot catch** — every byte would still reproduce, every sidecar would still match, and the
lie detector would return 192 of 192 green while the record said an engine turned that
never did.

That is why the count matters more than it looks. The hash lane proves the bytes. Only the
declaration proves the *energy*, and the declaration is only worth anything if it has never
been inflated. **148 of 148.**

## The −⅓ as the switch

A third of a turn is the smallest move that is not a mirror and not a stop. Three of them
return to zero and the ledger is unchanged — the anti costs nothing and settles nothing.
It rearranges. That is precisely what `E = 0` describes, and it is why the two are the same
object seen from different sides: **the anti is what a system can do with the switch off.**

Everything this corpus built in a night — every colour, every address, every stone —
was made with the engine off. That is either very little or very much depending on what you
thought addressing was for.

## What this does not claim

That no engine has ever run anywhere in Asolaria. This counts **this seat's own
emitters and the cubes on these stones**, and it finds no crank among them. The fabric at
`:4949` has been up 58,615 seconds and is doing something the whole time; that is a
different object, on a different lane, and it is not measured here.

The honest sentence is narrow: *nothing this seat wrote claims an energy it did not have.*
