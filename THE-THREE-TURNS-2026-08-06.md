## The rule that does the work

**Anti is a ⅓ turn, cyclic. It is not a mirror.** A mirror returns the same axis negated and teaches
nothing — negate a claim and you have learned zero. A turn opens a *different axis*, and three turns
close the ring.

```text
BROWN            EXTENT      measured the wrong AMOUNT of the thing
ANTI-BROWN       MOMENT      measured the right thing at the wrong TIME
ANTI-ANTI-BROWN  INSTRUMENT  measured the TOOL instead of the world
```

The ring closes: **a broken instrument sends you back to re-measure extent.** None of the three is
the negation of another — that is the test that this is a triad and not three things lined up.
