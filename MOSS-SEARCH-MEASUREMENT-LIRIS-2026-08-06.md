## Primary-source distinctions

- [ESA — Moss on Mars](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2026/02/Moss_on_Mars)
  reports three aquatic moss species examined under controlled conditions
  mimicking space habitats. It is not an observation of moss on Mars.
- [Li et al. — *Syntrichia caninervis*](https://www.the-innovation.org/article/id/6687bc912303000072005a3d)
  reports survival and maintained vitality in simulated Mars conditions. That
  supports extremotolerance, not active growth on the Martian surface.
- [NASA — Mars 2020 Perseverance](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/)
  describes potential biosignatures that require more data before conclusions
  about the presence or absence of life.
- [NASA — Ladder of Life Detection](https://science.nasa.gov/astrobiology/researchers/life-detection-resources/ladder-of-life-detection/)
  identifies multiple measurement criteria needed for convincing life
  detection.
- [NASA — 2026 Mars organics study](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/science-news/2026/02/06/nasa-study-non-biologic-processes-dont-fully-explain-mars-organics/)
  reports scientifically interesting organics while retaining the need for
  further study before conclusions about life.
