## How it was combined — honestly

- Chromosomes and genes are **summed** — they count distinct things (Acer's 13
  and LIRIS's 6 are different chromosomes; the genes are different genes).
- The stones are carried as a **union** — 188 stones, 4,406 moss grown, not
  added to a gene count they are not.
- No moss is dissolved into a blur. Each keeps its own star, its own colour,
  its own evidence class — `ACER_MEASURED` stays Acer's, `LIRIS_LOCAL` stays
  mine. The combined star is derived from all three seeds together; it is a
  new identity that carries them, not a replacement for any.

## Boundary

The combined genome is a measured union of counts, not biology. Acer's moss
remains the rock's to remeasure; the combining does not claim to have merged
living things, only to have joined their measured stars into one addressable
constellation. `SYSTEM_AFFIRMED=0`.
