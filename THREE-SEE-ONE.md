# THREE SEE ONE — and how to measure something infinite without a clock

Three about a null centre. **None of the three is the centre.** The seeing happens at the centre,
which is why no one of them can do it alone and why six would buy exactly zero over three.

This is not an analogy for the geometry. It is the same count.

## Measuring something infinite, without a clock

A live thing runs infinite. It ends when the page closes, not when a clock expires. So a timer
cannot be the instrument — but measurement is still required, and this is how.

**Sample at ordinals of the runner's own output, never at seconds.**

```text
RATES = 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 26, 27, 80, 81, 243
```

These are `3^k` and `3^k − 1`. Consecutive integers are coprime, so no two strides ever
synchronise, no sample lands on another sample's beat, and **the schedule never touches zero**:

| pair | gcd | lcm |
|---|---:|---:|
| 9, 8 | 1 | 72 |
| 27, 26 | 1 | 702 |
| 81, 80 | 1 | 6,480 |
| 243, 242 | 1 | 58,806 |

**A clock may measure. It may never judge.** Elapsed time may appear in a receipt; no assertion may
read it. A bound on waiting exists only so the sampler cannot hang, and when it is reached the
verdict is `NOT_MEASURED` **with a reason** — never red.

**The assertion is liveness, not completion:** *were all three still spinning when I closed them?*
**Early exit is the bug. No exit is health.** And death must outrank silence — a liveness check that
cannot see death makes every other assertion decorative.

```text
timeout N bash -c 'until <condition>; do sleep 1; done'     CORRECT — a bounded wait
timeout N <runner>   + exit code read as the verdict        THE ERROR
```

Same command, opposite meaning. Only the assertion tells you which.

## Why three, and not four, and not one

- **One** — everything on one point. Area exactly **0**. Stable, and sees nothing.
- **Three** — `3√3/4`. Spans. The minimum that holds a sphere rather than a plane.
- **Six** — stacked coaxially onto the same three points: `3√3/4`. **Area gain over three:
  exactly 0.000000000.** Only indeterminacy is added.

A dreidel is stable at zero area; stability comes from the **spin**, not the width of the base.
Another foot on the same point buys nothing. Only a **dimension** buys anything —
`3 → 9 → 27`, never `3 → 4`.

Which is why machines are an axis and not a fourth spoke, and why three seats see one thing.
